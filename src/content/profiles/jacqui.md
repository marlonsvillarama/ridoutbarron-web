Jacqueline is Ridout Barron's Wills and Estates lawyer and has been practicing in the area for more than 35 years. A proud third-generation Calgarian, Jacqueline comes from a family with deep roots in the legal profession.

She obtained both her Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and her LLB from the University of British Columbia. Jacqueline began her career in estates law in 1991 at the Office of the Official Guardian in Toronto before returning to Calgary.

Jacqueline's practice includes estate planning, probate and administration, guardianship, trusteeship, and estate litigation. She brings a practical and reassuring approach to work that often comes at a difficult or emotional time for clients.

Beyond the office, she enjoys spending time with her family and their standard poodle, Miss Eleanor Roosevelt Ridout, better known as Ellie. She and Peter enjoy traveling, watching films political debate, dog walking, the culinary and fermented arts, and occasionally, working together.
