Lilian joined Ridout Barron in 2025 after completing her Juris Doctor at the University of Alberta Faculty of Law. Before law school, she earned her Bachelor of Arts from the University of British Columbia.

While at the University of Alberta, Lilian spent three years volunteering with Student Legal Services of Edmonton. SHe worked with clients on civil, human rights, and family matters, gaining hands-on experience helping people navigate legal problems well before entering practice.

Lilian brings a thoughtful and practical approach to her work and enjoys making complicated legal issues easier for clients to understand.

Outside the office, Lilian is happiest outdoors. She enjoys spending time with animals, cycling when the weather cooperates, and working with textiles, particularly knitting and crocheting. Her most recent completed project was a sweater, which is probably the clearest proof that patience is not limited to her legal work.
