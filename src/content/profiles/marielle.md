Marielle is the Office Manager at Ridout Barron and has a hand in most things that keep the firm moving, from administration, human resources, and accounting, to the occasional mystery problem that lands on her desk. She is also the legal assistant for Wills & Estates and Litigation.

She has a background in human resources and corporate training, which comes in handy in an office where no two days look quite the same.

Marielle enjoys cooking and listening to music of almost any kind and is usually happiest when both are part of the evening.
