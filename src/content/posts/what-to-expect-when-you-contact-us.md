Reaching out to a law firm can feel like a big step, especially if you are already dealing with a stressful or unfamiliar situation. 

When you contact Ridout Barron, the first thing we will do is gather some basic information about you and the reason you are calling. This helps us understand what kind of matter you have and who at the firm may be best suited to assist. 

We may ask for the names of the people or businesses involved so that we can complete a conflict check before discussing the matter in detail. This is a standard part of the process and helps us confirm that we are able to speak with you about your situation. 

Depending on the type of matter, we may also ask you to send us documents before a lawyer reviews the file. Contracts, court documents, correspondence, purchase agreements, Wills, corporate records, or other background materials can often help us understand the issue more quickly and make the next conversation more useful. 

Once we have the information we need, we can let you know what the next step looks like. That may be arranging a consultation, sending you a questionnaire, requesting additional documents, or connecting you with the lawyer who handles that area of law. 

We will also let you know about any consultation fees, retainers, or other costs that apply before you are asked to proceed. 

You do not need to know the legal terminology or have everything perfectly organized before you call us. Start with what happened and what you need help with. We can take it from there. 

If something is on your mind and you are not sure whether you need a lawyer, tell us what’s going on. We will let you know whether we can help and what the next step should be.
