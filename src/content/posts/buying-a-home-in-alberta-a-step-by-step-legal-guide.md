<p class="summary">
Once your offer has been accepted, there are several legal and financial steps between signing the purchase contract and getting your name on title. Your lawyer handles much of this work behind the scenes, but knowing the general timeline can make the process easier to follow.
</p>

## Once your conditions are removed 

After any conditions in your purchase contract have been satisfied or waived, a copy of the final agreement should be sent to your lawyer. 

We review the contract and title to the property, identify any registrations that need to be addressed, and begin preparing for closing. Depending on the transaction, we may also review a Real Property Report, title insurance, condominium information, or other documents relating to the property. 

## About one to two weeks before possession 

If you are obtaining a mortgage, your lender will send instructions to our office. The timing varies between lenders, and we cannot prepare the final mortgage documents until those instructions arrive. 

Most lenders require approximately three clear business days to request and advance mortgage funds. You should therefore plan to be available to sign your purchase and mortgage documents before that funding window begins. 

If your possession date is approaching and you have travel or other commitments, let us know as early as possible so that we can coordinate your signing appointment. 

## Before your signing appointment 

Once we have the information needed to finalize the transaction, we will send you an email confirming the cash difference required to complete your purchase. 

The cash difference is the amount you need to provide after accounting for items such as your deposit, mortgage proceeds, property tax adjustments, legal fees, and registration costs. 

We will usually ask you to bring the cash difference to your signing appointment by bank draft, made payable in accordance with the instructions provided in our email. 

## A few days before possession 

You will meet with us to review and sign the purchase and mortgage documents. 

We will explain the documents you are signing, review the financial statement with you, confirm how title will be registered, and deal with any remaining requirements from your lender. 

You should also bring the required bank draft to this appointment so that the funds are available for closing. 

## Possession day 

On closing day, we coordinate with the seller’s lawyer and your lender to complete the financial and legal requirements of the transaction. 

Once the required closing conditions have been satisfied, the seller’s lawyer can authorize the release of keys. You can then collect them in accordance with the arrangements made through your realtor. 

Although you now have the keys, the legal work is not always finished at this stage. 

## After possession: Land Titles registration 

The Transfer of Land and, where applicable, your mortgage are submitted to the Alberta Land Titles Office for registration. 

Registration is not instantaneous. Processing times can vary throughout the year, particularly during periods of higher volume. As a conservative estimate, we recommend allowing approximately eight weeks for registration to be completed, although the actual timeframe may be shorter or longer. 

Once registration has been completed and we receive a clear title showing you as the registered owner, we will prepare and send you our client report. That report confirms that the transaction has been completed and includes the final documents relating to your purchase. 

Now you can get back to the important decisions, like where the couch goes and which box contains the coffee maker.
