</p>

Lawyers in Alberta are required by the Law Society of Alberta to collect certain information about the people and organizations they represent. These requirements help lawyers confirm who they are acting for and form part of the legal profession's safeguards against fraud, money laundering, and other illegal activity. 

**The first requirement lawyers have is client identification.** With limited exceptions, it applies whenever a lawyer is retained to provide legal services. For an individual, we are required to record information such as your full name, home address, telephone number, and occupation. If we are acting for a corporation or another organization, additional information is required. 

**The second requirement by the Law Society is verification.** This requirement generally applies when we are receiving, paying, or transferring funds on your behalf, although the Law Society rules contain several exceptions. When verification is required, we may also need to ask where the money for the transaction came from. 

Ridout Barron uses RealAML as our virtual identity verification partner. If verification is required for your matter, you may receive a secure request from RealAML asking you to provide government-issued photo identification and complete an identity check. The Law Society of Alberta permits lawyers to use approved technology-based methods to verify clients remotely.

RealAML is also ISO 27001 certified and SOC 2 Type I compliant. The company states that information collected for verification is not shared or used for another purpose. 

If we send you a RealAML request, completing it promptly helps us satisfy our professional obligations faster and keeps your matter moving.
