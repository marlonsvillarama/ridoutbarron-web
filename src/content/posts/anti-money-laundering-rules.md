Law firms often handle money on behalf of their clients, whether for a real estate transaction, a settlement, or the purchase or sale of a business. Because of that, Alberta lawyers must follow anti-money laundering rules set by the Law Society of Alberta. {.summary}

If your matter involves the receipt, payment, or transfer of funds, we may ask where the money came from. This is called source of funds information, and the answer may be as simple as personal savings, a mortgage, the sale of another property, an inheritance, or funds provided by another person. In some circumstances, we may also ask for supporting documentation. 

Our trust account can only be used for money that is directly connected to the legal work we are doing for you. It cannot be used simply as a convenient place to hold or transfer funds, and we may need to confirm the purpose of a payment before accepting or releasing it. 

Alberta lawyers are also generally prohibited from accepting more than $7,500 in cash for one client matter, subject to limited exceptions under the Law Society rules. 

Questions about a payment do not necessarily mean that anything is wrong. We are required to understand the transaction before we handle the funds, and we will let you know what information we need and why we need it. 

We know that questions about your money can feel personal, so our goal is to make the process as straightforward as possible while meeting the professional obligations that apply to Alberta lawyers.
