Adding or removing someone from the title to your property may sound straightforward, but a change in ownership can affect more than the Land Titles record. {.summary}

In Alberta, changing the registered ownership of a property generally requires a Transfer of Land. The current registered owners must sign the transfer, and the change is then submitted to the Alberta Land Titles Office for registration. 

> If there is a mortgage registered against the property, your first call should be to your lender. 

Your mortgage was approved based on the borrowers and ownership structure that existed when the loan was granted. Adding someone to title, removing an owner, or changing who holds an interest in the property may require the lender’s approval or additional documentation. 

Before contacting your lawyer, speak with your lender and explain the change you would like to make. Ask whether the lender will permit the change and whether there are any requirements that must be satisfied first. 

Once your lender has confirmed how they want to proceed, your lawyer can prepare the necessary transfer documents, review the existing title, and explain how the new ownership should be registered. 

It is also important to remember that Land Titles treats this as a transfer of ownership, even if no money is changing hands. That means Land Titles registration fees will apply when the transfer is submitted. The amount depends on the applicable Land Titles fee schedule and the value information required for the transfer. 

After the documents are signed, the lender’s requirements have been addressed, and the required registration fees are paid, the transfer can be submitted to Land Titles. Once registration is complete, the current title will reflect the new ownership structure. 

A title change is usually very manageable once the lender, ownership details, and registration requirements are sorted out. 

So, before anyone starts signing things at the kitchen table, give your lender a call and make sure they are on board. After that, we can take it from there.
