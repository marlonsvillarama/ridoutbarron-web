Peter's practice focuses on civil litigation with a particular focus on real estate litigation, construction law, and commercial litigation including shareholder's disputes and fraud.

Peter W. K. Ridout

Barrister & Solicitor, Partner

Peter is the managing partner of Ridout Barron and has been practicing law for more than 30 years.

He obtained his Bachelor of Arts from Trent University and his LLB from the University of British Columbia. Peter was called to the Ontario Bar in 1993 and the Alberta Bar in 1997.

His practice is focused on civil litigation, particularly real estate disputes, construction law, commercial litigation, shareholder disputes, and fraud. Over the course of his career, Peter has appeared at all levels of court in Ontario, as well as before the Alberta Court of King's Bench, the Alberta Court of Appeals, the Federal Court, and the Federal Court of Appeals.

Peter's approach to litigation is practical and direct, with an emphasis on understanding what the client is trying to achieve and finding the most sensible way to get there.

Outside the office, Peter enjoys spending time with his family, cycling, skiing, hiking, and following politics. He is generally happiest when there is either a trail, a hill, or a good argument involved.
